Description

All of these mods and datapacks are made by Dohst, please credit Dohst if you use any of them publicly.

Mods made by Dohst will only be available for the Fabric mod loader, on minecraft 1.20.1.

Datapacks will always be made for minecraft 1.20.1, and will likely be updated to 1.21.1 and maybe 1.21.11. New datapacks will not be backported to 1.19 or earlier.
